About Me

I am a final year B.E. Computer Science and Engineering student at Stanley College of Engineering and Technology for Women, Hyderabad. I am interested in data analysis: cleaning messy data, finding patterns in it and explaining what it means.

Web development is how I like to present that work. A good web page, like a good analysis, takes a lot of information and makes it easy to understand. I have also interned in cybersecurity, so I care about websites being safe as well as useful.

Outside of studies I enjoy baking, reading, writing, public speaking, and interior and fashion designing.
